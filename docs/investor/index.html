Real or luck?

Nine sales became fourteen. Is that anything?

Usually not. The tool says so out loud, and tells you how many days at this pace would settle it — instead of flattering you with a trend line.

An exact test, not a normal approximation. And it names the confounders a writer actually has: a promo, a review, a holiday, a price change.

Behind the scenes

A simulated store that proves the math.

70,000 fictional shoppers buy real public-domain titles for 92 days. Three experiments are planted with known answers, plus two checks on the checks: an A/A fixture that must find nothing, and a broken split that must be caught.

This is how the platform proves its own statistics — never a source for the library.

Decisions, written before the testSimulated
